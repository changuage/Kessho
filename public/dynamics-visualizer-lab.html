Degrade

Media wear, wobble, aliasing, corrosion, dropout, and tone loss.
A. Damage Map
Informative
Best forMain card
ReadsMost data
CPUMedium
Best Degrade default. In this prototype the rows are setting-derived estimates, not live audio telemetry. In production, wear/generation/alias/wow/flutter can be exact resolved target values; dropout/tone/noise can become live if the worklet reports them.
B. Tape Motion Spectral
Aesthetic
Best forFeel
ReadsWobble
CPUMedium
Best if Degrade should feel physical. The spectral wash shows tone loss and noise, while the green/amber traces separate slow wow from fast flutter. Pretty, but less exact than A.
C. Mod Matrix Constellation
Patch
Best forAdvanced
ReadsRoutes
CPUMedium
Best for advanced users. This explains modulation routing: which source is pushing wow, flutter, low-pass, wet, dropout, or alias. Great for editing the matrix, too busy for the main collapsed card.

What you’re currently listening to is

Kessho

Inspired by days of cloud gazing, where the visual landscape may feel familiar but is always changing, Kessho is his response to the idea of music as something concrete and cemented. What if music could be like air, like clouds, like memories—never the same twice, with subtle changes in detail?

Kessho grew out of his background in data science and a curiosity for how structure and chance can create music that continuously evolves. He composes the rules and relationships that guide the sound, and randomness operates within musical boundaries, opening space for unexpected combinations unique to the moment.

Patrick Chang

Point Clouds

Short Bio

Patrick Chang works with sound, field recordings and multisensory performance. A trained jazz drummer and data scientist, he explores how rhythm, place and guided randomness can shape listening. He is a co-founder of Nari Tea, where tea and sound become materials for gathering and shared attention.

Steeping Points

POSITIONING Symposium · Amos Rex, Helsinki

Amplified tea dripping · Rhythmic meditation

Commissioned by PUBLICS · Developed with Nari Tea

Co-developed a participatory tea performance for the POSITIONING Symposium. Brewing methods, duration and sensory cues invited participants to encounter tea through listening, smell, touch and bodily sensation, creating a space for shared attention within the symposium. Amplified sounds of dripping tea formed the basis for rhythmic meditation.

Kakegawa Chaennale

Kakegawa, Shizuoka, Japan

Local tea production recordings · Live composition

Developed a multisensory tea ceremony in a former academy, bringing together local tea culture, live sound and Miyako Morishita’s paintings incorporating tea leaves.
